The Problem

Energy inefficiency in buildings is a major global challenge.

Buildings account for approximately 40% of global energy consumption. Poor early-stage design decisions — such as suboptimal compactness, surface area, or glazing — lead to excessive heating and cooling demands that persist for decades. Traditional energy assessment tools require specialist expertise and are often inaccessible to early-stage designers and students.

The Solution

Instant ML-powered energy estimates — no specialist required.

EcoBuild provides an accessible, ML-based decision-support tool that instantly estimates Heating Load and Cooling Load from eight building parameters. Users can then run What-If Analysis to compare how design modifications affect energy performance — before a single brick is laid.

SDG 7 — Affordable and Clean Energy

EcoBuild directly supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 7 by helping architects, engineers, and students make informed, energy-efficient design decisions early — contributing to reduced operational energy demand and a more sustainable built environment.

Main Features

Building Analysis

Enter 8 building parameters and receive instant ML-based Heating Load and Cooling Load predictions.

What-If Analysis

Create named scenarios based on saved analyses and compare how parameter changes affect energy loads side-by-side.

Analytics & History

Review your saved analyses, track trends, and gain insight into your design patterns over time.

Dataset & Machine Learning

Built on a peer-reviewed benchmark dataset.

  • Dataset: UCI Energy Efficiency dataset (ENB2012) — 768 building simulations.
  • Model: Gradient Boosting Regressor trained separately for Heating Load and Cooling Load.
  • Heating Load — Test R²: 0.9984  |  MAE: 0.28 kWh/m²
  • Cooling Load — Test R²: 0.9889  |  MAE: 0.69 kWh/m²
  • Features: Relative Compactness, Surface Area, Wall Area, Roof Area, Overall Height, Orientation, Glazing Area, Glazing Area Distribution.

Academic Prototype Disclaimer: EcoBuild is an academic decision-support prototype. Predictions are estimates based on benchmark data and are not professional engineering certification or guaranteed real-world energy consumption.
