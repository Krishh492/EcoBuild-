Understanding the Eight Input Features

These parameters describe the building configuration used by EcoBuild to estimate heating and cooling loads.

Feature Type Range (Training Data) Description
Relative Compactness Continuous 0.62 – 0.98 Indicates how compact the building shape is. Higher compactness generally means less exposed outer surface.
Surface Area Continuous 514.5 – 808.5 m² Total external surface area of the building exposed to the surrounding environment.
Wall Area Continuous 245.0 – 416.5 m² Total area of the building's walls separating the indoor and outdoor environment.
Roof Area Continuous 110.25 – 220.5 m² Total roof surface area exposed to the outside environment.
Overall Height Continuous 3.5 – 7.0 m Overall vertical height of the building structure.
Orientation Discrete 2, 3, 4, 5 Represents the building's orientation. Different orientations can affect sunlight exposure and heat gain.
Glazing Area Continuous 0.0 – 0.4 (fraction) Proportion of the building surface occupied by windows or glass.
Glazing Area Distribution Discrete 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 Represents how the glazing or window area is distributed across the building's sides or orientations.
Note: Orientation and Glazing Area Distribution are categorical values encoded numerically in the ENB2012 dataset. A larger number does not necessarily mean a larger quantity.
